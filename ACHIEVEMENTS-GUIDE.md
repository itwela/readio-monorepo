# Lotus App Achievements Guide

This guide outlines the achievements you can unlock in the Lotus app based on your meditation practice and walking journey. Each achievement represents a milestone in your personal growth and wellness journey.

## Meditation (Presence) Achievements

### 1. 🌱 Mindful Beginner
- **Description**: Complete your first meditation session
- **Tracked via**: First entry in presence_stats
- **Value**: Marks the start of your mindfulness journey

### 2. 🔥 Presence Streak Master
- **Description**: Maintain a 7-day consecutive meditation streak
- **Tracked via**: presence_current_streak
- **Value**: Builds consistency in meditation practice

### 3. 🏆 Meditation Champion
- **Description**: Achieve a 30-day meditation streak
- **Tracked via**: presence_highest_streak
- **Value**: Demonstrates long-term commitment to mindfulness

## Walking (Giant Steps) Achievements

### 4. 🚶‍♂️ First Steps
- **Description**: Complete your first walking meditation session
- **Tracked via**: First entry in giant_steps_stats
- **Value**: Introduces mindful movement practice

### 5. ⚡ Walking Warrior
- **Description**: Maintain a 5-day consecutive walking streak
- **Tracked via**: giant_steps_current_streak
- **Value**: Establishes regular movement habits

### 6. 🌟 Step Master
- **Description**: Reach 100,000 total steps in walking meditation
- **Tracked via**: Cumulative steps in giant_steps_stats
- **Value**: Celebrates significant physical activity milestone

## Combined Achievements

### 7. 🎯 Double Discipline
- **Description**: Maintain both meditation and walking streaks for 3 consecutive days
- **Tracked via**: Simultaneous presence_current_streak and giant_steps_current_streak
- **Value**: Rewards holistic wellness practice

### 8. 💫 Wellness Explorer
- **Description**: Complete 50 combined sessions of meditation and walking
- **Tracked via**: Total entries in presence_stats and giant_steps_stats
- **Value**: Acknowledges exploration of different wellness practices

### 9. 🌈 Consistency King/Queen
- **Description**: Achieve highest streaks of 14+ days in both practices
- **Tracked via**: presence_highest_streak and giant_steps_highest_streak
- **Value**: Recognizes mastery in maintaining dual practices

### 10. 🎭 Balance Master
- **Description**: Complete equal numbers of meditation and walking sessions in a week
- **Tracked via**: Weekly count comparison between presence_stats and giant_steps_stats
- **Value**: Promotes balanced approach to mental and physical wellness

## Achievement Benefits

These achievements are designed to:
- Motivate consistent practice
- Celebrate milestones in your wellness journey
- Encourage balanced engagement with both meditation and walking
- Track your progress over time
- Build lasting healthy habits

Keep practicing and unlock these achievements as you progress in your wellness journey with Lotus!
