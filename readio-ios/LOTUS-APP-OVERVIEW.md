# Lotus App Overview

## What is Lotus?
Lotus is an innovative mobile application that combines AI-powered content generation with health and wellness features. The app serves as a personal content companion, offering users a unique blend of audio articles and physical activity tracking.

## Core Features

### AI-Generated Audio Content
- Transforms text content into engaging audio articles
- Utilizes advanced AI technology for natural-sounding voice synthesis
- Personalized content curation based on user preferences

### Step Tracking & Wellness
- Built-in step counter for daily activity monitoring
- Basic fitness tracking capabilities
- Integration with health metrics

## User Experience
- Clean, modern interface built with React Native and Expo
- Seamless audio playback controls
- Intuitive navigation and content discovery

## Technical Stack
- Frontend: React Native with Expo framework
- Authentication: Secure user management system
- Audio Processing: Advanced AI voice synthesis
- Data Storage: Cloud-based content management

## Target Audience
- Busy professionals seeking efficient content consumption
- Health-conscious individuals interested in activity tracking
- Users who prefer audio content over reading

## Business Model
Lotus operates on a freemium subscription model:
- Free tier: Basic step tracking features
- Paid tiers: Full access to AI-generated audio content and premium features

This overview provides context for understanding Lotus's functionality and business objectives, particularly useful for implementing features like payment processing and subscription management.
